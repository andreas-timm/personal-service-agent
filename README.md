# Personal Agent
A decentralized, open-source service agent for direct interaction with friends, contacts, and clients — operating personally on your behalf.

## Problem Solved
Mainstream Voice2Voice smartphone apps (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok Beta, Character.AI) do not support custom agents or audio stream injections. To address this, it is necessary to build and deploy an application on app marketplaces (Google Play, App Store) that enables continuous Voice2Voice communication with full customization.

## Personal Responsibility
To ensure trust and exceptional personalization, you are expected to maintain a degree of Personal Responsibility for your agent. Access is granted exclusively via personal invitation.

## Application Examples

### Delayed Communication
When you or your contacts are busy or unavailable, information can be passed to the agent for delayed retrieval. Contacts can request the information at their convenience.
Similarly, others can pass information to your agent, which you can receive either in its original form or optionally summarized or processed.

### Event Execution
- Visits, tours, and excursions
- Trainings and workshops
- Birthdays, team building
- Social gatherings, community services
- Live-action role-playing games (LARP)

### Real-Time Guidance
- Sport or martial arts exercises
- Meditation
- Mindfulness exercises
- Breathing practices
- Real-time coaching
- Disaster survival training

## Payment
1. **You should pay for the work of your agent yourself**
- Cloud services
- API services
- AI services

  _On your choice_

2. You can also offer a **subscription** or **usage fee** for additional or improved functionality.

## Tech Stack and Services (example)
- Initial framework: Pipecat
- AI: OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek
- Cloud: Google Cloud, AWS, Azure
- Services: Daily, Cartesia, Masumi

## Architecture
- Knowledge base and agent setting via **Obsidian Vault**
- Guidelines on how to start the agent
- Generate, sign, and upload your personalized app to the marketplace (Google Play, App Store).
